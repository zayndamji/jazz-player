# Piano samples

Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm, licensed under the
Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported License (CC BY 3.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Source: https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3

These files are the 128 kbps MP3 renderings published by the Tone.js
project (https://tonejs.github.io/audio/salamander/). They are used
unchanged, a subset of notes from A2 to A5.
