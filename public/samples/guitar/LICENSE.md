# Guitar samples

Nylon-string guitar from the Fluid (R3) General MIDI SoundFont by Frank Wen,
licensed under the MIT License (full text in LICENSE-FluidR3.txt).

These files are the MP3 renderings from midi-js-soundfonts by Benjamin
Gleitzman (https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts), FluidR3_GM,
instrument "acoustic_guitar_nylon". They are used unchanged, notes E2 to E5.
