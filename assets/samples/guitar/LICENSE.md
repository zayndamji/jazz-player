# Guitar samples

Spanish classical (nylon-string) guitar from FreePats, recorded in 2008 by
roberto@zenvoid.org for the FreePats project
(https://freepats.zenvoid.org/Guitar/acoustic-guitar.html, source files at
https://github.com/freepats/spanish-classical-guitar, version 2019-06-18).

Published under the Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal public domain dedication
(full text in LICENSE-CC0.txt, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

Changes: 21 of the notes, E2 to E5 about a whole step apart, retuned by their
measured offset (up to 14 cents), trimmed to 4 s with a 0.4 s fade, lowered
15.2 dB and encoded as MP3.
