# Bass samples

Acoustic bass from the Fluid (R3) General MIDI SoundFont by Frank Wen,
licensed under the MIT License (full text in LICENSE-FluidR3.txt).

These files are the MP3 renderings from midi-js-soundfonts by Benjamin
Gleitzman (https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts), FluidR3_GM,
instrument "acoustic_bass". Its README releases the Fluid renderings under the
Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 license
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/us/), and the repository itself
is MIT. They are used unchanged, notes E1 to E3.
